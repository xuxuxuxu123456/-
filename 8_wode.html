听闻将叹息折成蝴蝶时，
风会把它送往有回音的山谷

When a sigh is turned into a butterfly,
the wind will send it
to a valley with echoes

我的灵魂是只褪色的月亮纸船，
但你眼里有让它航行的海

My soul is a faded moon paper boat,
but in your eyes there is a sea
that allows it to sail.

我渴望着狂暴而巨大的欢乐，
即使再大的悲哀接踵而至，
我也在所不惜

I long for wild and immense joy,
even if the greatest sorrow
comes one after another,
I am willing to spare no effort
